Delantrás Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Delantrás is an App by JR Soft (“we” from here on) for taking photos and videos with your phone: with the front and back cameras at the same time (or one after the other), with manual controls and as a selfie camera, with filters and retouching.

1. Summary

  • Your photos and videos stay on your phone. There is no account or sign-up, and we have no server that receives your photos, your videos or anything you do in the App.
  • Your face does not leave your phone. Retouch, the smile shutter and background blur analyze the image on the phone itself, without storing data about your face and without identifying anyone (section 4).
  • These three features use Google libraries that send technical statistics to Google. That is why they are only turned on if you agree, and you can change this whenever you want (section 4).
  • The App shows Google AdMob ads, only in “Your photos”. For this, Google's SDK collects some device data on its own (section 6).
  • The App has no location permission and does not record where you take your photos. Only the ads SDK infers an approximate location from the IP address (section 6).
  • We do not use any analytics tool of our own.
  • We only receive what you write to us by email.

Sharing. When you share a photo or a video, you send it yourself to the app you choose from the Android menu. With “Share without location” (on by default), photos go out as a copy without the location, without the phone's make and model and without other hidden data, and videos go out without the location (on Android 12 or later). The original photo in your gallery is not touched.

Your gallery. Photos and videos are saved in the phone's normal gallery. If you have a cloud copy of your gallery turned on (for example, Google Photos or the manufacturer's), that service will also copy them, under its own terms. That does not depend on Delantrás.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: to take photos and videos. It is requested when you open the viewfinder.
  • Microphone: only for the sound of videos. It is requested the first time you record. If you do not grant it, videos are recorded without sound. The App does not listen to or record sound at any other time.
  • Storage: only on Android 8 and 9, to save to the gallery. From Android 10 onward it is not needed.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ads SDK (section 6).

The App does not request permissions for location, contacts or reading your photos: “Your photos” only shows what you have taken with Delantrás.

4. Selfie camera: retouch, smile and background blur

Everything is processed on the phone. Retouch (skin, light and shadows under the eyes), the smile shutter and background blur analyze the camera image only on your phone, frame by frame and in memory.

Face points and silhouette. To place the retouch and frame the shot, the App locates some points of the face (the outline, the eyes and the mouth). For the blur, it calculates the person's silhouette.

  • They are not stored: they are discarded with each frame.
  • They are not used to identify anyone and are not compared with other faces.
  • Nothing is inferred about anyone's sex, age, origin or emotions.
  • The smile is detected only to take the photo.

Nothing is sent. Neither your photos, nor your videos, nor the camera image, nor the points of your face, nor your silhouette are sent to JR Soft or to anyone else.

Google libraries. These features use two libraries from Google LLC that reach your phone with Google Play services:

  • ML Kit (face detection). If its model is not on your phone yet, Google Play services downloads it the first time you use retouch or smile, after “Turn on”.
  • LiteRT (the engine that runs the silhouette model, selfie_segmenter, which is included in the App). If it is not on your phone yet, Google Play services downloads LiteRT the first time you use background blur.

Google Play services is part of the phone's system and may already have these components, or download them on its own, because other apps and phone features use them too. Those downloads do not depend on Delantrás: the App does not use either of these libraries until you choose “Turn on”.

Images are processed on the phone and are not sent to Google. However, when they are used, these libraries send Google the following, encrypted:

  • device data (manufacturer, model, Android version and available accelerators) and App data (package name and version);
  • identifiers of this installation and of the device;
  • performance and usage metrics, configuration (for example, image format and resolution), input and output size, event type and error codes.

According to Google, it uses this data to measure, maintain, fix and improve these libraries, and to detect abuse, and ML Kit does not pass it on to third parties. LiteRT may also connect to Google to receive fixes, updated models and compatibility data for the phone's hardware.

More information:

  • ML Kit data: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure
  • ML Kit terms: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms
  • LiteRT in Google Play services: https://developers.google.com/edge/litert/android/play_services
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your choice. The first time you use one of these features, the App explains all this and asks you. With “Turn on”, the libraries are loaded. With “Not now”, they are not loaded, those three features stay off and the rest of the camera works the same. You can change this whenever you want in Settings → Privacy → Retouch, smile and background.

Maker effects. Some phones offer their own camera effects (Night, HDR, Portrait or Face retouch). When you choose them, they are applied by your phone's camera software, on the phone itself, not by Google's libraries.

5. Legal basis

  • Performance of the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): taking and saving your photos and videos, recording the sound of videos, applying filters and retouching, and analyzing the image on the phone for the features you choose, all on your phone.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 22(2) of Ley 34/2002, LSSI-CE, the Spanish Information Society Services Act):
    • for the technical statistics that Google's libraries send when you use retouch, smile or background blur (section 4);
    • for the ads SDK to store or read information on your phone and to show you personalized advertising. This consent is requested with Google's form in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. If you do not give it, Google may show you limited, non-personalized ads. To measure them and prevent fraud, Google and its partners rely on their legitimate interest, which you can object to in that same form.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): replying to the emails you send us.

6. Ads (Google AdMob) and third-party SDKs

Ads. The App only shows ads in “Your photos”, never in the camera viewfinder. It uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show ads and to ask for your consent the first time you open “Your photos”. These SDKs collect the following on their own, and share it with Google, to show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud:

  • the IP address, from which an approximate location is inferred;
  • device identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID;
  • your interactions with the App and with ads (for example, opens and taps);
  • diagnostics (App and SDK performance).

The App does not add anything from your photos, your videos or your face to ad requests.

To show and measure ads, Google's ads SDK (AdMob) collects device identifiers, as explained in this policy. Some US privacy laws may consider that “selling” or “sharing” personal data (see section 13, “US residents”).

More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Precise geolocation. The ad consent message is shown by Google with text that is common to all its advertising partners, which is why it mentions precise geolocation. This App has no location permission and does not pass GPS location to the SDK. According to Google, its ads SDK only uses the approximate location inferred from the IP address. You can refuse that use in “Manage options”.

Firebase Remote Config. The App includes it, turned off: it sends nothing unless it is turned on. If it is ever turned on, this policy will say so.

Google Play. “Rate the app” and the rating request inside the App use Google Play's ratings feature. Your rating is received by Google Play, under its own terms, not by us.

Google LLC may process in the United States the data collected by its SDKs and libraries. Google LLC participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, its UK Extension and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and also uses standard contractual clauses.

Our email is provided by Google (Gmail), with the same safeguards.

8. How to withdraw consent and control your data

  • Retouch, smile and background blur: in Settings → Privacy → Retouch, smile and background. If you turn it off, Google's libraries stop being used and those features turn off. Anything already sent is handled by Google according to its policy.
  • Ads: in the App, Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options (it appears where the law requires it: EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states). There you can withdraw your consent or object to legitimate interest, as easily as you gave it. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.
  • Microphone and camera: you can remove the App's permission in Android settings.
  • Your photos and videos: delete them from “Your photos” or from the gallery. The dual originals are deleted in Settings → Privacy or when you uninstall the App. Photos and videos in the gallery are not deleted when you uninstall the App.

9. Backups and switching phones

Android cloud backup (the one in your Google account) is turned off for this App's data: none of it is uploaded. Android does save the list of apps you had installed in your account, so it can reinstall them, but that list is managed by Google and does not contain App data. Your photos and videos are in the phone's gallery, not inside the App (section 2).

If, when setting up a new phone, you copy your data directly from the old one, with or without a cable, your App settings may move to the new phone. You request that transfer and Android carries it out, with its own tool or the phone manufacturer's, and under their own terms. According to Android's documentation, it goes from one phone to the other without passing through servers. In no case does it pass through ours: JR Soft does not receive any data.

Whether it happens depends on the Android version and the manufacturer (it does not happen on Android 8 or earlier), so we cannot guarantee it. If you set up the new phone from your Google account backup, without the old one, this App's data does not come back, because it is not in that backup.

What only works on the old phone is not transferred: the dual originals, the SDK identifiers and your choices about ads and about the Google libraries in section 4. On the new phone, the App will ask you again.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 16. Personalized ads and the statistics of the Google libraries require consent that children under that age cannot give on their own in the EU (Art. 8 GDPR; 14 in Spain, Art. 7 of the LOPDGDD, Spain's data protection act).

11. Retention

  • Your photos, videos and settings are kept on your phone until you delete them or uninstall the App (photos and videos in the gallery stay there even if you uninstall it).
  • The camera image, the face points and the silhouette are not kept: they are discarded with each frame.
  • The emails you send us: for as long as needed to reply to you and, at most, one year from the last reply.
  • The data collected by Google's SDKs and libraries is kept according to Google's policy.

12. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to us. You can also withdraw the consents you have given at any time, as explained in section 8; withdrawing them does not affect processing carried out before. Since we do not receive your photos or your videos, you exercise most of them directly on your phone (viewing, deleting or sharing what you have taken). For the data collected by its SDKs and libraries, Google handles your requests at https://myaccount.google.com and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (the Spanish Data Protection Agency, www.aepd.es) or with the data protection authority of the country where you live or work, or where the problem occurred.

13. US residents

If you live in a US state with its own privacy law (for example, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas or Virginia), you have the right to ask that your personal data not be sold or shared for targeted advertising. You can do this in the App: Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options → “Don't sell or share my data”. From then on, you will only see non-personalized ads. The App does not send sensitive data to anyone: your photos, your videos and your face data stay on your phone. For ads, Google's SDK uses device identifiers and an approximate location (city level). For any other request about your data, write to us at demual@gmail.com.

14. Changes

If this policy changes, we will say so in the App's release notes on Google Play and change the date above. If a new feature needs data that is not used today (for example, saving the location in photos), we will explain it to you in the App before turning it on and add it here.
